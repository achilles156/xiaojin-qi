About

I am an independent researcher and heritage conservation specialist with over a decade of fieldwork experience in historical cities and landscapes across China and Southeast Asia. My work brings together archaeology, urban and architectural history, historical geography, and heritage conservation.

Working closely with places, their material remains, and the people who inhabit them, I explore how historical environments have been shaped, understood, and transformed over time. I am particularly interested in connecting historical inquiry with the interpretation and conservation of cultural heritage.
