Independent Researcher

Urban History · Archaeology · Material Culture · Heritage Interpretation

I am an independent researcher working at the intersection of urban history, archaeology, material culture, and heritage studies, with a particular focus on historical cities and landscapes in China.

My research explores how material evidence, spatial practices, environmental conditions, and systems of knowledge shaped the historical built environment. I am also interested in how archaeological and historical research can contribute to new approaches to heritage interpretation.
